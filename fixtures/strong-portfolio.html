About me

I moved into software from civil engineering in 2021 after automating a survey workflow that had eaten two days a week of my team's time. Since then I have shipped production TypeScript at a logistics startup, contributed accessibility fixes to two open-source design systems, and mentored eleven people through their first deployments. I care most about the unglamorous parts: migrations that do not lose data, error states that explain themselves, and interfaces that work with a keyboard.

Outside work I maintain a small library for parsing GPS traces and I am slowly learning Rust by rewriting my own tools in it. I am looking for a team that reviews code carefully and ships often.

Selected projects

Fleetwise — route optimisation dashboard

A dashboard that plans daily delivery routes for a 40-vehicle fleet. I built the scheduling service in Node.js and Postgres, cut the nightly optimisation run from 14 minutes to 40 seconds by replacing nested queries with a single recursive CTE, and added a keyboard-driven dispatch view the operators actually use.

Trace — GPS trace parser

A zero-dependency parser for GPX and FIT files, published to npm with 4,000 weekly downloads. Written in TypeScript with a streaming API so a 200 MB ride file parses in constant memory. I wrote the fuzz tests that found three malformed-input crashes in the previous implementation.

Quietroom — accessible group chat

A small real-time chat app built to test how far you can get with server-sent events instead of WebSockets. Screen-reader users can follow a conversation without losing their place because new messages are announced through a polite live region rather than moving focus. Deployed on Fly.io behind a Redis pub/sub fan-out.
